QUAAA 2027

Quality and alignment of AI-generated mechanizations

April 10, 2027 · Copenhagen, Denmark · co-located with ETAPS 2027

About

Generative AI agents combined with proof assistants now produce mechanized results, in mathematics, computer science, and beyond, at a scale the theorem-proving community struggles to absorb and of widely varying quality. This informal workshop aims to distill best practices for improving the mechanizations’ quality and their alignment with the human formalizer’s goals.

By quality we mean reusability, maintainability, readability, idiomatic style, and good use of existing libraries. By alignment we mean ensuring that formal statements faithfully capture their informal counterparts, such as theorems or specifications, and understanding what alignment should mean when no such counterpart exists.

Call for Submissions

We invite submissions presenting:

  • exemplary (or instructively flawed) autoformalizations,
  • techniques and tools for steering agents, or
  • metrics and benchmarks for assessing quality and alignment.
